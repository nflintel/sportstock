

## Sports Investment Landing Page — "SportStock"

A dark-themed, modern landing page for a fantasy sports stock exchange platform where users can trade athlete shares like stocks.

### Sections to Build

1. **Header/Navbar** — Logo, navigation links (Trade, Market, About, FAQ), and a "Sign Up" CTA button. Sticky, dark background with subtle transparency.

2. **Hero Section** — Large headline "The New Way to Invest in Sports", subtitle text, "Start Trading" CTA button, and a decorative illustration area with gradient effects (pink/cyan/purple glow aesthetic).

3. **Player Cards Carousel** — Horizontal scrolling cards showing athlete profiles with: avatar, sport badge (NBA/NFL/MLB), name, team, stock price, and price change (green for positive). Auto-scrolling carousel.

4. **"How Does It Work" Section** — 3-step process with icons: Deposit Funds → Watch the Market → Make a Trade. Clean layout with connecting visual elements.

5. **"Buy Low, Sell High" Section** — Split layout with illustration on one side and feature list on the other (Players value, Invest, Trade from Anywhere) with small icons.

6. **Features Grid** — "Play all sports in one place" heading with 4 feature cards: Secure Payments, Transparent, Multi-Sports, 24/7 Support. Each with an icon.

7. **Referral CTA Section** — "Invite Friends, Get Free Stock" banner with gradient background and CTA button.

8. **Testimonials Section** — Carousel of user testimonials with avatar, name, location, and quote.

9. **FAQ Accordion** — Expandable questions about the platform with "Ask a Question" CTA.

10. **Footer CTA + Footer** — "Experience the future of fantasy sports" banner, then footer with links and social icons.

### Design System
- **Dark theme**: Deep navy/dark blue background (#1a1b4b → #0f1035 gradients)
- **Accent colors**: Vibrant pink/magenta (#ff2d78), cyan (#00f0ff), purple gradients
- **Cards**: Semi-transparent dark cards with subtle borders and glow effects
- **Typography**: Bold, clean white headings, muted gray body text
- **Animations**: Subtle hover effects, smooth scroll, carousel auto-play

### Technical Approach
- Single-page React app with smooth scroll navigation
- Embla Carousel for player cards and testimonials
- Radix Accordion for FAQ section
- All static/mock data (no backend needed)
- Fully responsive design
- Placeholder illustrations using gradients and icons from Lucide
